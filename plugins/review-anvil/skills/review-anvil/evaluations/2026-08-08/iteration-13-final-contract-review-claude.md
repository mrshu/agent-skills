## Verdict

**Keep `a5830fc`.** The evidence supports it specifically and non-trivially: on the initial audit Claude alone would have shipped `3520340907` and `3652735574`, and Sol alone would have shipped `3161743720` (`iteration-13-production-path-action-audit-claude-retry.json` vs `-sol-retry.json`; union of 5 in `iteration-13-production-path-final-comments.json:13-19`). The single-auditor design it replaced would have passed defects that the union caught. Caveats: the new fail-closed branches (missing/malformed/timed-out rows, COMMENT forcing) were never exercised end to end in this run, and I6 shows the per-ID validation rule is under-specified enough to have fired spuriously on this very evidence.

The assessment is unusually candid about its main limits (gate narrower than whole-comment fact preservation, `:101-103`; not evidence for arbitrary raw comments, `:12-14,120-122`; inline-only, `:179-180`). The overclaims are narrower but real: "exactly" (I1), the 47-corpus tallies (I3), the asymmetric corpus argument (I4), and a broken citation (I7). No contamination overclaim found — the freeze rule, hash lock, and the post-selection-not-temporal framing at `:31-35` are consistent with the artifacts (all 27 captured RAV reviews predate the iteration-12 selection commit `2da4796`, 2026-08-11T01:22:36Z; latest review 2026-08-11T00:57:37Z in `iteration-13-top-level-review-artifacts.json`). No model-independence overclaim found; if anything the artifacts show auditor verdicts are strongly model-dependent (union overlap 2 of 5), and the contract is safe because it fails closed, not because auditors agree — worth saying out loud in the assessment.

**Most important next experiment — not the assessment's pick.** It proposes structured predicate/fact IDs through synthesis (`:166-169`). That is a large build aimed at a diagnostic measured entirely out of domain. The binding gap is that *no valid evidence exists for this contract on review-anvil synthesized findings after the contract was locked* — the one in-domain corpus was burned, and the assessment's own "strong on synthesized findings" (`:12`) rests on iteration 12's self-declared non-generalizing 47-comment set (`iteration-12-assessment.md:76`).

Do this instead, and it does not require waiting for new public comments: generate the in-domain corpus by running the engine. Pick unseen PRs, run review-only to produce synthesized findings, freeze the findings plus their exact requested-work prose *before* rendering, lock the renderer-contract hash as in `iteration-13-untouched-final-corpus.json:5-6`, then render, audit, and judge. Add one mechanical precondition that closes C2: build `rendered_requested_work` as verbatim spans of the emitted body and assert the spans cover every request/boundary sentence in it, failing closed otherwise. Defer the scanability work (`iteration-13-assessment.md:170-172`) until in-domain numbers exist — the −0.227 was measured against raw human prose containing suggestion blocks the renderer discards (I5), so it is partly an artifact of the corpus, not of preservation density.
